# Privacy Policy for TinyTastes

**Last updated:** April 18, 2026

Thank you for using TinyTaste ("we", "our", "the app"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

## 1. Information We Collect

TinyTaste is designed to work without collecting personal information. We do **not** require you to create an account, and we do **not** ask for your name, email address, or phone number.

The only data handled by the app falls into these limited categories:

### a. Purchase Information
When you purchase a subscription or in-app purchase, our payment provider **RevenueCat** processes the transaction on our behalf. RevenueCat receives:
- **Purchase History** — which products you purchased and your subscription status.
- **Device ID** — a pseudonymous identifier used to restore and validate your purchases.

This information is used solely to deliver and manage your purchase. It is **not** linked to your identity and is **not** used for advertising or tracking across apps.

### b. Data Stored On Your Device
All food entries, preferences, and app settings you create in TinyTastes are stored **locally on your device**. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

## 2. How We Use Information

The limited data described above is used only to:
- Process and restore in-app purchases and subscriptions.
- Provide the core functionality of the app on your device.

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party service:

- **RevenueCat** — handles in-app purchases and subscription management. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

- **Apple App Store** — processes payments and provides the platform. See Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## 4. Children's Privacy

TinyTaste is intended to help parents and caregivers track their child's food journey. The app does **not** knowingly collect any personal information from children. All content is entered by the parent or guardian and stored locally on the device.

## 5. Data Security

Because TinyTaste stores your data on your device, the security of that data depends on the security of your device. We recommend using a device passcode and keeping your operating system up to date.

## 6. Your Rights

You can delete all data at any time by deleting the app from your device. For questions about purchases, you can manage or cancel your subscription through your Apple ID settings.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
